Vector Database Cost Modeling Across Scale

Managed vector databases typically charge per pod-hour or per million vector-dimensions stored in memory. For lightly-queried indexes, always-on RAM pods create high fixed monthly bills even when query volume is low.

Storage Footprint Calculation

A 384-dimensional float32 vector requires 1,536 bytes of raw storage. Including SQLite B-tree indexes, text payloads, and metadata overhead, 100,000 vectors occupy roughly 250 MB on disk. At 1,000,000 vectors the footprint is ~2.5 GB, and at 10,000,000 vectors it reaches ~25 GB.

Self-Hosted vs Managed Economics

On a single cloud VM with attached SSD storage, 100K and 1M vector indexes run comfortably on a $12/mo to $24/mo instance. By contrast, a dedicated always-on managed pod with replication starts around $70/mo and scales past $400/mo at 10M vectors.
